# The Ticket has owners: one for its record and classification, one for its transitions

`tickets.ts` owns the Ticket: the `Status:` vocabulary, the line format and its two commit messages, the record, and the classification as one exhaustive `STATUS_CLASS: Record<Status, StatusClass>` (blocked | startable | in-flight | merged) read through `isBlocked`/`isStartable`/`isInFlight`/`isMerged`, with `leftoverInFlight` and `blockersMerged` going through that map. `transitions.ts` owns every edge a Ticket can take — `beginTicket`, `markResolving`, `settleAfterAgent`, `settleAfterConflict`, `openUnblocked`, `recoverLeftover` — and its header is the transition picture: each edge, in order, with the transaction that wraps it. `main-writes.ts` keeps the lock and the raw writes (ADR-0042).

It replaces three overlapping classifications with nothing joining them: the eight statuses declared in one place, four of them listed as in-flight in `tickets.ts`, two as startable in `pick.ts`'s gate. Measured before the change: adding a ninth status compiled with no error and left it neither in flight nor startable, so a Ticket holding it disappeared from the drain silently. Now the classification is a `Record<Status, …>` and a new status fails `tsc` until someone classifies it — the compiler asks the question the literals used to swallow.

A verb opens the transaction around a route of its own; the two that run inside a drain sweep (`openUnblocked` under pick, `recoverLeftover` under the leftover pass) assert the caller's and say so. The sixteen Main-write sites remain sixteen; nine now live inside the owner. The three forwarding paths (`ticket-line.ts`, `begin.ts`, `settle.ts`) are deleted and their five importers name the owners.

Rejected: leaving the classification beside pick's gate and tickets' in-flight set; a classification map without predicates so each caller compares strings; verbs that do not state their transaction stance (the stance is what ADR-0042 makes load-bearing); keeping the forwarding paths "for one release" (nothing outside the pack imports them); moving the lock into the transition owner (main-writes stays the only lock taker); deriving a Ticket's class from its Status by convention instead of a table the compiler checks.
